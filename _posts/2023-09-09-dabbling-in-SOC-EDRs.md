Taking a brief break from AppSec, although I don't plan on becoming a SOC analyst, I stumbled upon this excellent tutorial by Eric Capuano on how to quickly get up to speed for SOC work.

It was a fun exercise for an afternoon, and I definitely recommend checking it out.

- [Intro](https://blog.ecapuano.com/p/so-you-want-to-be-a-soc-analyst-intro){:target="_blank"} - So you want to be a SOC Analyst? Intro
- [Part 1](https://blog.ecapuano.com/p/so-you-want-to-be-a-soc-analyst-part){:target="_blank"} - Set up a small virtualization environment (2 small VMs)
- [Part 2](https://blog.ecapuano.com/p/so-you-want-to-be-a-soc-analyst-part-ea2){:target="_blank"} - Put on your adversary hat, it's time to make (and observe) some noise
- [Part 3](https://blog.ecapuano.com/p/so-you-want-to-be-a-soc-analyst-part-77e){:target="_blank"} - Emulating an adversary for crafting detections
- [Part 4](https://blog.ecapuano.com/p/so-you-want-to-be-a-soc-analyst-part-1e0?sd=pf){:target="_blank"} - Blocking an attack
