Update January 2025: GitBook is great, but it has increased my dependency on the cloud instead of allowing me to manage my notes locally. I will start migrating them back to Jekyll.

Though I am a huge fan of Jekyll and its minimalist look and feel, using it for all CTF writeups and InfoSec notes could make things a bit crowded. I've found the WYSIWYG editor of GitBook to be far more comfortable for focusing on content, with less concern about image responsiveness, post layout, Markdown editors, and the like.

To streamline and categorize my content, I am relocating older posts to GitBook under [notes.jawad.ca](https://notes.jawad.ca){:target="_blank"}, creating a dedicated space for in-depth technical insights. The primary blog will continue to host more generic posts.

Key Advantages of the Move
- **Editorial Simplicity:** GitBook's WYSIWYG editor ensures a straightforward and distraction-free writing process.
- **Automated Backups:** GitBook's built-in Git repository backup functionality provides a secure backup solution.
- **Efficient Search Functionality:** GitBook offers indexed and effective search functionality for easy content retrieval.
- **SEO Optimization:** GitBook's platform enhances search engine optimization, improving the discoverability of my technical content
