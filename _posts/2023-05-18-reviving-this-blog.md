Hello, World... again! I've been meaning to document my learning journey on this blog for a while, but I always found myself not doing so due to a mix of laziness and [imposter syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impostor_syndrome){:target="_blank"}. However, I believe now is the right time to overcome these challenges and resume sharing my experiences.

Throughout my career in the software development lifecycle, I have worn multiple hats, but my curiosity has consistently been drawn towards the infoSec aspect of the industry. With this in mind, I have made the decision to delve into the basics in detail which will be the main focus of my upcoming blog posts. My goal is to not only achieve my career aspirations but also enhance the security of the products I work on.

Through the documentation and sharing of my journey, my intention is to solidify my own understanding but I also hope to contribute to the knowledge and growth of others in the field. 

**Disclaimer:** I will be utilizing [LLMs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model){:target="_blank"} to improve and enhance my written texts, thereby saving valuable time that would otherwise be spent on reviewing my grammar mistakes.
